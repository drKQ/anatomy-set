# The Anatomy Set

The free practice companion to *Medical Terminology* by Brian Khan Quintana.

One page, no account, no install. It opens on any phone and keeps your progress on that device. There are twenty-four chapters, one for each chapter of the book. Short rounds teach the word parts, then spaced review brings each word back just before you would forget it.

**The game:** https://drkq.github.io/anatomy-set/

**Practice:** https://drkq.github.io/anatomy-set/practice.html. Every chapter's must-know words in the author's own voice, plus the book's matching, build-the-word, read-the-chart and self-check exercises.

Built for students at Southwestern College, Cuyamaca College and Palomar College.

Tip: add the page to your Home Screen so your progress stays safe on an iPhone.

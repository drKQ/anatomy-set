Receipt checker

For instructors. Paste receipts below, one per line (straight from Canvas Text Entry submissions). Each line is checked against the app's own checksum, parsed, and flagged for duplicates and stale dates. Then download the CSV for your gradebook. Everything runs in this page; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

What a receipt is evidence of. A receipt shows the app was genuinely used on that device — parts held, this chapter's accuracy, the Shift score with its attempt count, the cold-read score (unseen words decoded — the honest transfer number), and rounds of spaced review completed. It is participation evidence, not a proctored score: anyone who can open a browser console can forge one, which is why the guidance is full credit for any genuine receipt at a small weight, with graded assessment in Canvas.
